Background

About Me

I'm a Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering graduate (Honours) currently completing my Master's degree at RMIT University, Melbourne. My work spans computational fluid dynamics, structural analysis, and hands-on fabrication — from submarine hydrodynamics to performance car components.

During my time at Ghenova Engineering I worked in the naval shipbuilding sector producing production-grade engineering drawings, 3D models, and GD&T documentation across the HMAS Hunter and Arafura class programs using CATIA V5, FORAN, and Windchill PLM.

Outside the office I'm building a topology-optimised strut brace for my CLA 45 AMG — the full CAD → FEA → fabrication loop. That project is documented as it progresses.
